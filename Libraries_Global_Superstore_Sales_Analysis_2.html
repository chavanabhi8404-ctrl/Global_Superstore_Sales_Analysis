Global Superstore Sales Analysis¶

Project domain: Data Analytics and Business Intelligence¶

Project Objective:¶

  • To analyze the Sample Superstore sales dataset using Python and data visualization techniques to identify sales trends, profit patterns, customer behavior, and business performance.

Skills Used in the Project:¶

  1. Python – For data analysis and calculations.
  2. Pandas – For data cleaning and manipulation.
  3. NumPy – For numerical operations.
  4. Matplotlib – For creating charts and graphs.
  5. Seaborn – For statistical data visualization.
  6. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – For identifying patterns and trends.
  7. Data Visualization – For presenting sales and profit insights.
  8. Business Analysis – For understanding business performance.

Dataset Description:¶

  • Dataset Name: Sample Superstore Sales Dataset
  • Records: 9,994
  • Features: 21
  • Target Variables: Sales and Profit

Key Attributes:¶

  • Order Details: Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date, Ship Mode
  • Customer Details: Customer ID, Customer Name, Segment
  • Product Details: Category, Sub-Category, Product Name
  • Sales Information: Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit
  • Location Details: Country, City, State, Region, Postal Code

Q1. What is the distribution of Sales?¶

In [27]:
plt.figure(figsize=(8,5))
sns.histplot(df['Sales'], bins=50, kde=True)
plt.title("Distribution of Sales")
plt.xlabel("Sales")
plt.ylabel("Frequency")
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • Sales distribution is right-skewed.
  • Most transactions have relatively low sales values.

Conclusion:¶

  • Most sales transactions generate smaller revenue, while a few high-value orders contribute significantly to total sales.

Q5. What is the correlation among Sales, Profit, Quantity and Discount?¶

In [22]:
corr = df[['Sales','Profit','Quantity','Discount']].corr()

plt.figure(figsize=(8,5))
sns.heatmap(corr,annot=True,cmap='coolwarm')
plt.title("Correlation Heatmap")
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • Discount and Profit have a negative correlation (approximately -0.22).
  • Quantity has a weak positive correlation with Profit.

Conclusion:¶

  • Sales have a positive relationship with Profit, while higher discounts tend to be associated with lower profitability.

Q6. What is the relationship between Sales and Profit?¶

In [30]:
sns.scatterplot(data=df, x='Sales', y='Profit')
plt.title("Sales vs Profit")
plt.xlabel("Sales")
plt.ylabel("Profit")
plt.show()

Analysis:¶

  • The scatter plot shows the relationship between Sales and Profit.
  • Most points show a positive relationship.

Conclusion:¶

  • Higher sales do not always guarantee higher profit. The company should focus on profitable sales.
